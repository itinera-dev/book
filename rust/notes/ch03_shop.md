Services and rescue steps are not in itinera yet: their designs are agreed in the specification's proposals for [services](https://github.com/itinera-dev/spec/issues/95) and [rescue steps](https://github.com/itinera-dev/spec/issues/3), and this program waits in `pending/` until itinera implements them, so it is neither compiled nor run. It is written against the designs as they stand. A step asks for a service as a parameter of `run` marked `#[service]`, such as `#[service] carrier: &dyn Carrier`, borrowed for the attempt only. A provider is a method of the workflow's `#[workflow]` block marked `#[provider]`, for one instance per journey, or `#[provider(per_attempt)]`, for one per attempt; it returns `Result<Box<dyn Carrier>, Error>`, and may request the attempt number with `#[attempt]`. The syntax of the rescue step is not settled; this program assumes `rescue = RenewCard` on the `#[with_step(...)]` line, and a field marked `#[reason]` through which the rescue step reads why the step it rescues failed.
