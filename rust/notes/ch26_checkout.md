Services and rescue steps are not in itinera yet, so this program waits in `pending/` and is neither compiled nor run; it is written against the agreed designs of [services](https://github.com/itinera-dev/spec/issues/95) and [rescue steps](https://github.com/itinera-dev/spec/issues/3). The syntax of the rescue step is assumed: `rescue = RenewCard` on the `#[with_step(...)]` line, and a field marked `#[reason]` for why the rescued step failed. That field is an `Option<Reason>`, because a charge that ends in an abnormal termination has no reason, only an error.
