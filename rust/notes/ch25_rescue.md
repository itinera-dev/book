Rescue steps are not in the Rust crate yet, so this example is not compiled, and its syntax may still change. A rescue step is an ordinary step type, deriving `Step` with a `#[step]` block, and the workflow names it on the step it rescues with `rescue = RenewCard` in that step's `#[with_step(...)]` line; the executor names it `charge_rescue`. Its field marked `#[reason]`, an `Option<Reason>`, receives the failure's reason from the step it rescues, as a hook's `#[reason]` parameter does, or `None` when that step ended in an abnormal termination, which has no reason. A `#[with_step]` line that gives both `rescue` and a `retry_budget` above 0 is expected not to compile, as the other mistakes the macros can see in a declaration do, and a rescue step that skips may become impossible to write.
