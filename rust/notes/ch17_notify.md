A workflow policy's `impl` block carries `#[workflow_policy(name = "...")]`, and its hooks are marked `#[on_workflow_success]` and `#[on_workflow_failure]`. Each returns `Result<(), Error>`, since a workflow hook makes no decision, and an `Err` aborts the journey with `hook failed`. Its parameters may be `#[from_workflow]`, `#[journey_id]`, `#[role]`, `#[contributor]` and `#[reporter]`; asking for `#[reason]` or `#[from_step]` in a workflow hook does not compile. The workflow lists its policies in `#[workflow(..., policies = [Notify])]`. The policy is built with `Default`, or with a function of its block marked `#[factory]`.
