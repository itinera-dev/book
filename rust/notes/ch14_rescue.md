Rescue steps are not in `itinera` yet: they are agreed in [spec#3](https://github.com/itinera-dev/spec/issues/3) and will come in a later release. Until then, this chapter's example is not compiled, and its syntax may change. It assumes that a step's line in the workflow names its rescue step with `rescue = RenewCard`, and that a rescue step's struct asks for the failed attempt's reason with a field marked `#[reason]`, as a step hook does with a parameter. The field is an `Option<Reason>`: a rescue step also runs after an abnormal termination, which has no reason, and then receives `None`. The rescue step is otherwise an ordinary step: it derives `Step`, and its `run` takes the same handles as any other.

A retry budget on a step that has a rescue step is expected to be a compile error, as the other mistakes the macros can see in a declaration are, and a rescue step that skips may become impossible to write.
